#### Systems & Engineering Work

- **[Multimodal Meeting Summarizer](https://github.com/rahulrajesh21/Multimodal-Meeting-Summarizer)**  
  Neural multimodal fusion pipeline combining WhisperX audio diarization, MFCC acoustic modeling, ResNet-50 visual slide analysis, and DistilBERT for automated meeting segmentation and key highlight extraction. Evaluated on the AMI Meeting Corpus.

- **[Frigate Discord Notifier](https://github.com/rahulrajesh21/Frigate-Discord-Notifier)**  
  High-resolution automated video clip extraction and notification bridge from Frigate NVR to Discord channels with configurable event filtering and asynchronous webhook dispatching.

- **[QueueCTL](https://github.com/rahulrajesh21/QueueCTL)**  
  Resilient background job orchestration engine in Node.js featuring durable queues, dead-letter queue (DLQ) isolation, operational telemetry, and exponential backoff retry policies.

- **[Privacy Prediction](https://github.com/rahulrajesh21/privacy_prediction)**  
  Graph Neural Network (GNN) framework for quantifying user privacy leakage and risk attribution across social graphs with explainable reporting.
